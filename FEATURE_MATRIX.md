## Evidence rules

Quoted from LensMate-Goal.md section 1.3 (Evidence levels), which Standards section 7.3 applies:

> Every result is labelled with exactly one evidence level:
>
> - **Simulator:** the virtual glasses replaying recorded inputs
> - **Emulator:** the Android emulator or the iOS Simulator
> - **Real phone:** an owner-run measurement on a physical phone (the owner's iPhone 15 first; Android phones later)
> - **Bench hardware:** owner-run tests on electronics on the bench (P0, P1)
> - **First wearable prototype:** owner-run tests on the first wearable (P2)
> - **Integrated advanced wearable:** owner-run tests on the daily-use prototype (P3 and later)
> - **Commercially prepared hardware:** owner-run and facility tests on product hardware (P4)
>
> A lower level never satisfies a requirement that needs a higher one. Simulator and emulator results never satisfy real-phone acceptance gates.

The simulator is not hardware evidence (Standards 7.3, C07). Simulator results never prove the indicator, microphone-disable, thermal, battery, optical, acoustic, ergonomic or electrical behaviour; checklist 39 needs physical hardware.

Other rules applied in this file:

- Mock provider behaviour is labelled **MOCK**. MOCK results are not measured AI performance.
- No row is marked Tested, Measured or Integrated for a real phone or device. No row claims real-phone, emulator or device evidence that does not exist.
- Status uses the Goal section 6 vocabulary: Specified, Unverified, Implemented, Tested, Measured, READY_FOR_OWNER, Hardware-pending, Blocked, Integrated, NOT_DUE. NOT_DUE rows are excluded from every percentage.
- P-1 scope follows Goal section 8 (In scope): F1 to F6, F11, F13, F20 to F22, F26, F27, F33, F35, F40, F50 basics, F53 basics, session and gesture rules, provider gateway, privacy and retention, the simulator, and the measurement harness. The profile switch framework (U1) is counted because checklist items 41 to 43 need it.
